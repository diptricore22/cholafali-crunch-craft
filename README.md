# Manohar's Crispy Legacy

Create a premium, modern, highly responsive website for **Manohar Cholafali Center®**, a legendary Ahmedabad street-food/snack brand known for its crispy Cholafali and signature chutney.

## Brand Information

**Business Name:** Manohar Cholafali Center®
**Tagline:** Since 1984
**Location:** Shop No. 4, Shree Vijay Apartment, Under Metro Bridge, Vijay Cross Road, Opp. Metro Pillar No. 171, Navrangpura, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380009
**Phone / WhatsApp:** +91 94279 50278
**Business Type:** Traditional Gujarati Street Food / Fast Food
**Speciality:** Crispy Cholafali with special chutney
**Price:** Affordable / approximately ₹100–₹200
**Google Rating:** 4.7 ★ from 568+ reviews
**Known For:** Fresh, crispy Cholafali, spicy/flavorful chutney, quality, service
**Opening:** Approximately 11:00 AM – 9:00/9:30 PM

Do not invent additional business facts, awards, branches, menu items, or claims that are not provided.

# Overall Design Direction

Build a website that feels like:

* Premium Indian food brand
* Modern Ahmedabad street-food identity
* Heritage + contemporary design
* Bold food photography
* Warm, appetizing visual language
* Minimal but expressive
* Mobile-first
* Fast and polished
* Easy to order/contact

The design should take inspiration from the visual quality of premium brands such as modern restaurant websites, but **do not copy any existing website**.

Avoid making it look like a generic restaurant template.

The key brand story should be:

**“A beloved Ahmedabad Cholafali destination with a legacy dating back to 1984.”**

Use the heritage as a major visual element without making the website look old-fashioned.

# Important Design Rule

The website must NOT look like:

* A generic food delivery website
* A basic WordPress restaurant template
* An overly colorful Indian restaurant website
* A corporate website
* An old-fashioned Gujarati restaurant website

It should look like a **premium modern food brand website built for a famous Ahmedabad street-food institution**.

Think:

**Heritage street food × modern editorial design × premium food photography × strong mobile ordering UX.**

The final result should feel polished enough that the business owner could use it as their official website immediately.

Before finishing, carefully check:

* Mobile responsiveness
* Tablet responsiveness
* Desktop layout
* Typography
* Spacing
* CTA consistency
* Accessibility
* SEO
* No overflow
* No broken images
* No fake links
* No invented business claims
* No placeholder lorem ipsum

Make the final UI visually impressive, modern, warm, premium and conversion-focused.
